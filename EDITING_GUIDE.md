# RES² Website Editing Guide

Work on one page at a time in this order:

1. Home — `app/page.tsx`
2. Research — `app/research/page.tsx`
3. About — `app/about/page.tsx`
4. People — `app/people/page.tsx`
5. Coursework — `app/coursework/page.tsx`
6. Interactive Tools — `app/tools/page.tsx` and `app/tool-lab.tsx`
7. Engineering Education — `app/education/page.tsx`
8. Student Success — `app/student-success/page.tsx`
9. News — `app/news/page.tsx`
10. Awards & Recognition — `app/awards/page.tsx`
11. Consultancy — `app/consultancy/page.tsx`

## Simple review workflow

For every page:

1. Open the page and read it as a visitor.
2. Verify every fact, date, course number, name, and external link.
3. List changes in plain language. You do not need to edit the code yourself.
4. Provide approved photographs or graphics when relevant.
5. Review the updated page on desktop and mobile.
6. Mark the page approved before continuing.

To work with ChatGPT, say: **“Let’s edit the Research page.”** The page can then be reviewed one question at a time.

## Adding a news item

Provide:

- publication or event date;
- category;
- headline;
- two- or three-sentence summary;
- reliable external link, when available;
- one approved image and its caption, when available.

Add the newest entry first in the `news` list inside `app/news/page.tsx`.
